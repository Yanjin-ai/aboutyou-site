帮助

换手机了，记录怎么带过去？

记录只在手机里。换机前在 App 里「导出」，用 iPhone 的「快速开始」或 iCloud 整机备份迁移时，App 数据也会跟着一起迁移。

看不到步数和睡眠

去 iPhone「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备 → About You」，确认「步数」「睡眠」都打开。睡眠需要戴 Apple Watch 睡觉，或在「健康」里设置了睡眠时间表，手机才有数据。

按了麦克风没有反应

About You 只用手机本地的语音识别。去「设置 → 通用 → 键盘」打开「听写」，并确认已下载你说的语言；然后在「设置 → About You」里打开麦克风和语音识别。

提醒太多 / 太少

App 里「设置 → 提醒」可以改时间、改频率，或者全部关掉。做过的事当天不会再提醒，也永远不会提醒你「几天没记了」。

小组件不更新

长按桌面小组件 → 移除，再重新添加一次。

需要找人聊聊

About You 不是心理咨询或医疗服务。如果你正处在困难里，App 里「设置 → 需要找人聊聊」有你所在地区的热线；也可以访问 findahelpline.com。

Support

Moving to a new iPhone

Your entries live only on your phone. Use Export in the app before switching; migrating with Quick Start or a full iCloud device backup also brings app data across.

Steps or sleep don't show up

Go to Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → About You and turn on Steps and Sleep. Sleep data needs an Apple Watch worn to bed or a sleep schedule set up in Health.

The microphone does nothing

About You only uses on-device speech recognition. Turn on Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard and make sure your language is downloaded, then allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings → About You.

Too many or too few reminders

In the app, Settings → Reminders lets you change the time and frequency, or turn them off. You won't be reminded about something you've already done that day, and never about “days missed.”

Widget isn't updating

Long-press the widget, remove it, and add it again.

Need someone to talk to

About You is not counseling or a medical service. If you're going through a hard time, Settings → Talk to someone in the app lists helplines for your region, or visit findahelpline.com.
