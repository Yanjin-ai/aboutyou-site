隐私政策

About You · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

一句话：About You 不收集你的任何数据。你记下的一切只存在你自己的手机里，我们看不到，也不上传。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。App 没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计、没有广告、没有任何第三方 SDK，也不发起任何网络请求。

你的记录存在哪里

你记下的事情、能量、时刻和备注，保存在你手机上 App 自己的存储空间里（包括给桌面小组件共用的那一块）。删除 App 后，这些数据会随之删除。我们没有副本，无法帮你找回，所以请在需要时使用 App 内的「导出」。

「健康」数据

如果你在 App 里打开「苹果健康」，App 会只读你的步数和睡眠时长，用来在你的记录旁边显示，帮助你自己对照。这些数据只在你的手机上使用：不上传、不分享、不用于广告或营销，也不会写回「健康」。你可以随时在 iPhone 的「设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备 → About You」里关闭。

语音

用说话的方式记录时，App 使用 iPhone 系统的设备端语音识别。系统弹窗里「可能发送给 Apple」是通用模板文字；About You 强制要求在手机上识别，如果手机上没有对应语言的识别模型，就不会录音。录音不保存。

通知

提醒都是手机本地安排的通知，不经过任何服务器。通知里不会出现你写下的原话。

导出与分享

只有在你主动点「导出」或「分享」时，App 才会生成文件或图片，交给系统的分享面板，发到哪里由你决定。

儿童

App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

变更

如果以后加入需要联网的功能（例如可选的云备份），我们会先更新本页并在 App 内明确告知，并且只在你主动开启后才会生效。

Privacy Policy

About You · Effective November 1, 2026

In short: About You collects no data. Everything you log stays on your own iPhone. We can't see it, and nothing is uploaded.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests.

Where your entries live

Your entries, energy ratings, moments and notes are stored in the app's own storage on your phone (including a shared area used by the home-screen widget). Deleting the app deletes them. We have no copy and cannot restore it, so please use Export in the app when you want a backup.

Health data

If you turn on Apple Health in the app, it reads only your step count and sleep duration to show next to your entries. This data is used only on your phone: it is never uploaded, shared, or used for advertising or marketing, and nothing is written back to Health. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → About You.

Voice

Voice entries use Apple's on-device speech recognition. The system prompt mentions sending data to Apple because it is a generic template; About You requires on-device recognition and will not record if your phone lacks the on-device model for your language. Audio is not saved.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. Your own words never appear in a notification.

Export and sharing

Files or images are created only when you tap Export or Share, and handed to the system share sheet. Where they go is your choice.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we ever add a feature that needs the internet (such as an optional cloud backup), we will update this page and tell you clearly in the app first, and it will only work after you turn it on.
